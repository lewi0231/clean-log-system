# User Story 001: Landing Page Hero Section

## Overview

The hero section is the first thing visitors see when they land on the Tally Runner website. It must immediately communicate the value proposition, target the right audience, and guide users toward the primary call-to-action.

## User Story

**As a** small business owner (tradesperson, car detailer, or service contractor)  
**I want to** immediately understand what Tally Runner does and how it can help my business  
**So that** I can quickly determine if this solution is right for me and take the next step

## Acceptance Criteria

### 1. Hero Headline

- Headline clearly communicates the core value proposition in 8-12 words
- Uses language that resonates with small business owners (not technical jargon)
- Mentions key benefit: job management, invoicing, or worker coordination
- Example: "Manage Your Field Workers, Jobs, and Invoicing in One Place"

### 2. Hero Subheadline

- Expands on the headline with 1-2 sentences (15-25 words)
- Addresses the target audience directly (tradespeople, car detailers, service businesses)
- Highlights the pain point being solved
- Example: "Streamline your service business with automated invoicing, mobile job tracking, and flexible pricing rules designed for tradespeople and detailers."

### 3. Primary CTA Button

- Prominent, high-contrast button above the fold
- Action-oriented text: "Start Free Trial" or "Get Started Free"
- Positioned prominently (left side on desktop, centered on mobile)
- Secondary CTA option: "Watch Demo" or "See How It Works"

### 4. Hero Visual

- Shows the product in action (dashboard screenshot or mobile app mockup)
- Demonstrates key features visually (job list, invoice, mobile form)
- Professional, clean design that builds trust
- Responsive: adapts well to mobile devices
- Alternative: Animated illustration showing the workflow

### 5. Trust Indicators

- Brief social proof near CTA: "Join 100+ service businesses" or similar
- Security badges if applicable (SSL, data protection)
- "No credit card required" if offering free trial

### 6. Mobile Responsiveness

- Hero section fully visible on mobile without scrolling
- CTA buttons easily tappable (minimum 44x44px)
- Text readable without zooming
- Visual scales appropriately

## Design Principles (Based on Research)

1. **Clarity Over Cleverness**: Direct, benefit-focused messaging
2. **Visual Hierarchy**: CTA is the most prominent element after headline
3. **Above-the-Fold**: All critical elements visible without scrolling
4. **Progressive Disclosure**: Hero introduces concept, details come later
5. **Accessibility**: High contrast, readable fonts, keyboard navigable

## Content Guidelines

### Headline Options

- "Field Service Management Made Simple"
- "Stop Chasing Paperwork. Start Growing Your Business."
- "The All-in-One Platform for Service Businesses"
- "Manage Jobs, Workers, and Invoices from Anywhere"

### Subheadline Focus Areas

- Time savings (automated invoicing)
- Mobile-first (workers use app in field)
- Flexibility (custom pricing rules)
- Industry fit (tradespeople, detailers)

### CTA Variations

- Primary: "Start Free Trial" (most common)
- Alternative: "Get Started Free"
- Secondary: "Watch Demo" or "See Pricing"

## Technical Requirements

- Load time: Hero section visible within 1 second
- Image optimization: WebP format with fallbacks
- Lazy loading: Below-fold content loads after hero
- A/B testing: Support for headline/CTA variations

## Success Metrics

- Hero section engagement rate
- CTA click-through rate
- Time spent on page (indicates interest)
- Bounce rate from hero section
